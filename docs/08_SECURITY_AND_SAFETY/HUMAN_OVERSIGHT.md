# Human review and safety boundary

WHO or national guidance based outputs are drafts for clinician review. The current test suggestions show the supporting facts and source; the doctor can change the requested tests before saving the laboratory handoff. The blood-pressure prompt asks staff to recheck and alert the doctor; it is not a complete triage decision.

The workflow does not diagnose or label a patient normal, and it does not choose medicines, doses, or alternatives. A doctor records the medication order and the pharmacist records what was dispensed and the instructions provided. Medication recommendation and substitution support remains disabled because no verified hospital formulary/stock list and supporting patient-safety context are configured.

Local demo roles and audit metadata support workflow testing, but they are not a production authorization system. Use synthetic records in this demo. Clinical validation and facility-approved protocols are required before clinical use.
